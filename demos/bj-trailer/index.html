About the shop

Southwest Side since 1980.

B.J. Trailer & Truck Repair has been on Chicago's Southwest Side since 1980, just off I-55 and Central Ave. Easy in from the Stevenson, easy out to the intermodal yards and the beverage routes.

We repair, modify, custom-design, buy, sell and lease trailers, containers and chassis. Seven trailer bays, eight mobile trucks and twenty-five technicians handle everything from a roadside truck start to a full beverage-body refurbishment.

Ask for Andy or Damian. They'll give you a straight answer and a same-day estimate.
