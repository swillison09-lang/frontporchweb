Meet Jordan Serna!

Hey there! I'm Jordan, your go-to specialist for curly hair and the proud owner of Salon Phoenix. I'm here to help you embrace your natural curls and unlock their true beauty. With personalized styling routines and a whole lot of love, we'll make your curls shine like never before.

I've been through the phase of not fully loving my own curls, but now my mission is to empower you to embrace and fall head over heels in love with yours. We'll work together to find the perfect styling routine that fits seamlessly into your life and helps you achieve all your curly hair goals, whether it's defined ringlets, soft waves, or bouncy coils.

When you visit Salon Phoenix, you'll step into a warm and welcoming environment where your unique beauty is celebrated. I'm committed to building a community that embraces diversity, self-love, and acceptance. So, let's laugh, chat, and have a blast while we transform your curls into something truly amazing.

When I'm not transforming hair, you'll find me exploring local restaurants, wineries, and indulging in the vibrant atmosphere of farmers markets. Live music fills my soul, and I'm always up for a dance party! I'm also a proud dog mom to Mia. She's my sidekick and brings so much joy to my life.

So come join me at Salon Phoenix, where we celebrate your unique curls and let your inner beauty shine brighter than ever. Together, we'll embark on an exciting journey of self-discovery, confidence, and a whole lot of curly hair love.
