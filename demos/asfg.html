Our Purpose

To provide professional and personal support, information sharing, education, and friendship to help elevate the client experience and expertise at member businesses.

Who We Are

A national membership group of fiduciary financial advisors across the United States. Our members are peer-selected via an application process. Our members hold various credentials and perform financial, tax, and/or investment planning services for high net worth clients.

Client types may include

  • Closely-held businesses
  • Executives
  • Athletes
  • Entertainers
  • Artists
  • Entrepreneurs
  • Retirement plans
  • Endowments
  • And others

The experience

Leveraging our combined expertise and broadening our depths of knowledge.

We convene

Twice a year, in June and January

Twice a year, typically in June and in January, the group meets in person for a lively educational meeting, time to manage the business items of the group, and time to learn more about each other.

Group members alternate planning these meetings to make them worthwhile as a travel and time commitment is made. Group members with commonalities or geographical proximity may choose to connect more frequently during the year, and this is encouraged! Having trusted peers to support you across the country is important and valuable.

See A Recent Meeting Agenda
We connect

A private LinkedIn group

Since we are from all parts of the country, a private LinkedIn Group has been established to facilitate communication about various topics or questions our members may have. Allowing ongoing dialogue and idea sharing is critical to our members finding value in the ASFG.

We contribute

Our time and expertise

Our members contribute to the group, with their time and expertise. To cultivate and grow the ASFG, it is important that members feel connected, welcome, and supported. It is important that each member be willing to give to the group so that all may benefit.
